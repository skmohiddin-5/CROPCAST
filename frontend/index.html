02About this project

This prototype combines live weather information with a machine-learning model. Rain probabilities are estimates, not official forecasts. Farm suggestions are general and should be checked against local advisories. The animated weather ambience (sun, rain, snow, clouds) can be switched on the dashboard, and quick tools let you flip °C/°F, dim the screen, go full screen, or copy a text summary — all saved locally in your browser.
